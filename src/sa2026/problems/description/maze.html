The Maze Problem

Session 2 · Describing a problem: states, actions, goal and cost

The question

A player stands on the start cell S of a grid maze. Help the player reach the goal cell G.

  • In one move, the player goes one cell up, down, left or right.
  • The player can not walk into a wall and can not leave the maze.
  • Every move costs 1.

Your job in this session: write the state, the actions and the problem in Java. The search algorithm comes in the next sessions.

Check yourself

  
1. How many different states does this maze have?

  
2. From the start, which actions are allowed?
Only Move down and Move right. Up and left go outside the maze.

  
3. Why are the walls not part of the state?
Because they never change. A state keeps only what can change: the player position.

  
4. What is the smallest number of moves to reach G?

  
5. What would change if some cells were "mud" and cost 3?
Only the cost. The path with the fewest moves may not be the cheapest any more. We will need an algorithm that looks at the cost (Uniform Cost Search).
